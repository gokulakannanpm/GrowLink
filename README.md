## 🚀 About GrowLink

College student information is often fragmented across different records and systems.

Academic marks, attendance, activities, courses, certifications, skills, and mentor observations may exist separately, making it difficult to understand a student's complete development journey.

**GrowLink connects that story.**

It provides a shared student-development layer where mentors can:

- View assigned students through a unified profile
- Monitor academic performance and attendance
- Review skills and extracurricular activities
- Identify areas requiring attention
- Record mentor observations
- Create follow-up actions
- Schedule mentoring sessions
- Track student progress over time

Students get their own personalized view of their development journey.
