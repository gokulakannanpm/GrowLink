## 🌱 What is GrowLink?

Students generate valuable development data every day:

- 📚 Academic performance
- 📝 Assessments
- 📊 Attendance
- 💻 Skills & courses
- 🏆 Projects & hackathons
- 🎯 Extracurricular activities

But this information is often fragmented across different sources.

Mentors may see marks in one place, attendance somewhere else, and activities somewhere else.

### GrowLink brings these signals together.

Instead of asking:

> **"Where is this student's data?"**

GrowLink helps mentors ask:

> **"What does this student's overall development look like, and what should we follow up on?"**

# 🎯 The Problem

### Students are more than their marks.

A student's development spans multiple dimensions:

| Academic | Attendance | Skills | Activities |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Assessments | Attendance | Courses | Projects |
| CGPA | Consistency | Certifications | Hackathons |
| Subject Progress | Engagement | Technical Skills | Events |

The problem is that these signals are often **disconnected**.

### This creates a simple chain:

**Fragmented Data → Limited Visibility → Delayed Intervention**

GrowLink is designed to close that gap.

---

# 💡 The Solution

## One shared development view.

### 👨‍🎓 For Students

Students can:

- View their academic progress
- Track attendance
- Monitor skills and courses
- Track projects and activities
- View mentor recommendations
- Understand areas requiring attention

### 👨‍🏫 For Mentors

Mentors can:

- View assigned students
- See academic + attendance + activity information together
- Add mentor notes
- Create mentor actions
- Schedule follow-ups
- Review student development
- Conduct structured weekly reviews

### 🏫 For Institutions

GrowLink can provide a structured foundation for:

- Student mentoring
- Counselling workflows
- Development tracking
- Future ERP/LMS integration
